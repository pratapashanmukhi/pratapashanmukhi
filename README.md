### ✦ About Me

I am a 3rd-year **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)** student at **VIT-AP University** with an academic CGPA of **7.69**.

I am passionate about developing intelligent applications and solving complex, real-world challenges through Artificial Intelligence. My work balances experimental deep learning research with clean, resilient software engineering, focusing on autonomous AI agents, semantic retrieval pipelines, and scalable models.

**Key Technical Interests:**
- ▹ **Machine Learning & Deep Learning** — Supervised and unsupervised modeling, feature engineering, and neural network architectures
- ▹ **Generative AI & LLMs** — Structured prompt reasoning, domain adaptation, and context-extended architectures
- ▹ **AI Agents & Workflows** — Dynamic tool-calling, autonomous multi-agent coordination, and persistent state management
- ▹ **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** — Hybrid dense/sparse vector retrieval, reranking, and semantic indexing
- ▹ **NLP & Computer Vision** — Text sentiment classification, vision transformers, and image pathology detection
- ▹ **AI-Powered Application Development** — End-to-end full-stack architectures, asynchronous backends, and low-latency APIs
